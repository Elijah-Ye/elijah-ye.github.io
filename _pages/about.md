Hi! 👋

My name is Elijah Gaohan Ye. `Elijah` is my English first name, and `Gaohan` is my Chinese first name. I am a hardware engineer working on design-for-test (DFT), design verification, and computer architecture. I earned my Master of Science in [Electrical and Computer Engineering](https://ece.illinois.edu/) from the [University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign](https://illinois.edu/) in May 2026 (GPA 3.86/4.00), and my Bachelor of Science in Computer Engineering from the same institution in December 2023 (GPA 3.96/4.00).

While at UIUC, I was a Teaching Assistant for Digital Systems Laboratory (ECE 385) and Computer System Engineering (ECE 391).

<h4 id="my_experience"><code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge"> $cat my_experience </code></h4>

Most recently, I was a DFT Engineer Intern at [Astera Labs](https://www.asteralabs.com) on the Leo CXL Memory Controller. I inserted memory BIST and repair across 186 SRAM macros, debugged ATPG, MBIST, and boundary-scan gate-level simulation failures, and built Python toolchains that automate die-to-package remapping of BSDL and STIL test patterns.

Before that, I spent two internships at [Rivian Automotive](https://rivian.com) as a Design Verification Intern on the RAP1 Silicon Team, where I developed system-level testcases for [RAP1 (Rivian Autonomy Processor)](https://rivian.com/newsroom/article/rivian-unveils-custom-silicon-next-gen-autonomy-platform-deep-ai-integration), a custom 5nm AI inference chip delivering 1600 sparse TOPS for autonomous driving compute. I enhanced verification coverage, developed system-level and FuSa tests, and worked with UVM testbenches.

My research focuses on computer architecture, particularly hardware-software co-design for accelerating Large Language Models (LLMs), advised by Prof. Nam Sung Kim. I have published work on "Exploiting Intel Advanced Matrix Extensions (AMX) for LLM Inference" in IEEE Computer Architecture Letters (CAL) 2024, which received the **IEEE Best Paper Award**, and co-authored "ReScue: Reliable and secure CXL memory" at HPCA 2026. My work on transparent memory management for large-scale LLM training and tuning is currently under review.
